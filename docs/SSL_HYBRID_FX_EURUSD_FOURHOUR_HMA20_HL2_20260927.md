This slice exposed two price-grid errors and a FIFO gap-fill lifecycle gap in the broker emulator. An average entry price a few binary ULPs above a five-decimal FX tick caused a touched stop to be missed; outward snapping of an off-grid stop could produce a float one ULP above the exact bar high. Tick snapping now reconstructs representable decimal tick prices. A Monday gap made two short profit targets marketable at the same open. TradingView executed the deeper target first and attributed its fill to the oldest open trades under [default FIFO](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/v5/concepts/strategies/), then executed the remaining exit even though its named entry had already closed. The local broker now reproduces that target priority, FIFO trade attribution, and pending-order lifecycle. Focused regressions cover the exact FX tick touches, deeper target ordering, and FIFO allocation; the broker test subset passes (524 tests).
